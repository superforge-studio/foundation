## The setup (`START-HERE.md`, `workbench/setup/`), a starting point

- **Claude offers to drive the owner's own browser** for every step that's
  theirs on a website: Railway's account and settings, GitHub's secrets,
  the key, and the domain's DNS. With Claude in Chrome it works in their
  Chrome, where they're already logged in; they still log in where a site
  asks, and type every password, code, card number and key themselves.
  It's offered, matched to how much they know, never assumed
  (`START-HERE.md`, "Across every step"; `3-ship.md`).
- **A domain's DNS is found before the owner is asked**: its nameservers
  (`dig +short NS`) name who answers for it, and `whois` its registrar, so
  Claude can say which site to log in to (`4-make-it-theirs.md`, "A
  domain, if there is one").
- **The business's name comes from the portal's prompt**, which now says
  it, so `2-taste.md` confirms it rather than asking.
