# 0.38.0 (2026-11-26): Meta's click ID isn't an ad's, and the remember decision is one function

Two follow-ups to Skylevel 0.27.0's review of how visits are tracked. The
docs said a visit's `ad_network` names whose ad was clicked, which Meta's
doesn't: its click ID is on every tap out of Facebook and Instagram. And
the decision whether the site remembers a visitor, which `recordVisit`
made inside itself, is a function of its own now, `decideVisitor`, which
`recordVisit` calls and a route that sets the `visitor` cookie before it
keeps the events calls too, where it had to rebuild it. A minor, since it
adds an export. Nothing the app does changes: no schema change (only a
comment in `schema/foundation.ts`, which no migration follows), no
variable, and nothing for the owner.

## Meta's click ID (`source/backend/visits/`, `source/frontend/visitors/`), machinery

- **`ad_network`**: `source/backend/visits/README.md`'s table of a
  visit's columns, and the comment on `analytics_visits`' campaign
  columns in `source/database/schema/foundation.ts`, said it's whose ad
  was clicked. For `google`, `microsoft`, `tiktok` and `linkedin` it is,
  since only an ad's click carries their IDs (`gclid`, `gbraid`, `wbraid`,
  `msclkid`, `ttclid`, `li_fat_id`). Meta's, `fbclid`, rides on every tap
  out of Facebook and Instagram, a post's as much as an ad's, so `meta`
  says only that the visit came from one of them, and a Meta ad is told
  apart by the `utm_` tags on its link. Both say so now: the README's
  table names the column "the ad network whose click ID the landing's
  address carried", with a paragraph after it.
- **The same words where the IDs are kept and read**: `summarize.ts`'s
  comment on `ad_network`, `source/frontend/visitors/describe.ts`'s
  `CLICK_IDS` and the landing's properties, and
  `source/frontend/visitors/README.md`'s `view` row, which says "a click
  ID" where it said "an ad's". What's recorded and kept is the same:
  `fbclid` still makes a visit's `ad_network` `meta`.

**A copy with code of its own that reads `ad_network`**, like a report of
paid visits or a lead's source, reads `meta` as a visit from Facebook or
Instagram, not an ad's click: a Meta visit is paid only by its `utm_`
tags, as Skylevel 0.27.0's channels count it.

## `decideVisitor` (`source/backend/visits/visitors.ts`), machinery

- **What `recordVisit` decided of a visitor** before it kept their events,
  never a bot, then `whomToRemember` by the site's mode, their answer and
  their country, and only under an ID their cookie holds, or would, is
  `decideVisitor(locate, { mode, visitor })`. It answers what they're on
  (`browser`, null for a bot), where they are (`place`, read once, and
  never for a bot), the ID they're remembered under (`remembered_id`,
  null when they aren't) and whether their page asks them (`ask`).
  `recordVisit` calls it in place of the lines it had (`record.ts`), and
  answers as it did.
- **Why**: a route that sets the `visitor` cookie before it keeps a
  visitor's events, like Skylevel's short-link redirect, which sets it on
  the redirect itself, had to rebuild the decision from `describeBrowser`
  and `whomToRemember`, and a release that changed `recordVisit`'s would
  have parted the two. Calling `decideVisitor` keeps them one.
- **`followAnswer`'s comment** (`cookies.ts`) said every call that keeps a
  visitor's events follows what `recordVisit` answered, which a route that
  decides first doesn't: it names both ways now. The README's "A page's
  events" says it too.
- **`source/backend/visits/tests/visitors.test.ts`** tries it: a bot is
  neither remembered, asked nor placed; anyone else is placed once and
  remembered under their ID as `whomToRemember` says; and nobody is
  remembered without an ID.

**A copy that decides whether the site remembers a visitor itself**, from
`describeBrowser` and `whomToRemember`, calls `decideVisitor` in its
place, and keeps its events by the same `place`
(`locate: () => decided.place`), so the visitor is placed once.

**How to tell a copy has it**: `source/backend/visits/visitors.ts`
exports `decideVisitor`, and `source/backend/visits/README.md` says
"`ad_network` says whose ad was clicked for every network but Meta".
