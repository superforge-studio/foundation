# 0.34.0 (2026-11-25)

The site's analytics is kept for good. Until now an event was deleted 90
days after it happened and a visit after 25 months, so any report over
events could look back 90 days at most, and a copy that shows what
someone did on the site beside their messages would lose their visits
while their emails and texts stay.
An event is about 250 bytes with its indexes, roughly 120 MB a year for a
site with 5,000 visits a month, so keeping it costs little. A minor,
since it changes how long a copy keeps what it already has. No schema
change and no variable.

## How long the analytics is kept (`source/utilities/settings/index.ts`, `source/backend/visits/prune.ts`), machinery

- **`TEMPLATE_SETTINGS.keep_events_for` and `keep_visits_for` are
  `"forever"`** (were `90 * DAY` and `760 * DAY`). The hourly
  `prune-analytics` runner deletes nothing more as the template ships;
  `deleteOldEvents` and `deleteOldVisits` stay, for a copy that sets a
  duration.
- **A remembered visitor's IP address still goes after 90 days**, now by
  a setting of its own: `keep_ip_addresses_for` is required in
  `Settings`, `90 * DAY` in `TEMPLATE_SETTINGS`, and `prune.ts`'s
  `KEEP_IP_ADDRESSES_FOR` reads it alone, with no fallback to the
  events'. Before, it was as long as the events unless set, which would
  have kept every address for good with them.
- **The tests** (`source/backend/visits/tests/prune.test.ts`): the
  template's settings are checked as above, a new test runs the runner
  with them and finds only the addresses blanked, and the tests of the
  deleting pass their durations (`LIMITED`, was `SHIPPED`) rather than
  read the app's.
- **The docs**: `source/backend/visits/README.md`, "How long it's kept",
  says why, what it costs, and how a copy keeps less (25 months,
  `760 * DAY`, for a business that keeps to France's regulator, CNIL);
  `schema/foundation.ts`'s comment on `analytics_events` says so too.
- **`START-HERE.md`'s question on remembering visitors** (step 1, item
  13), a starting point: it tells the owner every visit is kept for good,
  with what was viewed and clicked on it. A copy has already answered it.

Flags:

- **Owner**: from this release's deploy, nothing more is deleted: events
  and visits already deleted are gone, so the history grows from what the
  copy has. An owner whose privacy notice says how long the analytics is
  kept, or who keeps to CNIL's 25 months, keeps less by setting
  `keep_events_for` and `keep_visits_for` (`760 * DAY` for 25 months) in
  `source/utilities/settings/app.ts`; the agent asks before the update
  merges.
- **A copy that set `keep_events_for`** in `utilities/settings/app.ts`
  without `keep_ip_addresses_for` had its addresses kept as long as its
  events. It now keeps them 90 days, the template's: set
  `keep_ip_addresses_for` in `app.ts` to keep them as long as before.

How to tell a copy already has it: `keep_events_for: "forever"` in
`source/utilities/settings/index.ts`'s `TEMPLATE_SETTINGS`.
