# 0.33.0 (2026-11-25)

Foundation's license is no longer MIT. From this release on it's the
Superforge Foundation License, from VITALIZE DIGITAL LLC, doing business
as Superforge. A business builds its own software on its own copy, may
have anyone it hires work on that copy, and runs what it builds and
sells what that does. Nobody passes Foundation on as a template, starter
kit or boilerplate, or builds on it for someone else from a copy of
their own. The credit in every page's source, which the docs called a
request, is a term. MIT let anyone republish the template as their own,
which is what the new terms stop. A minor, since it changes the terms a
copy is under. No code a copy runs changes: no schema, no variable.

**Owner**: read `LICENSE` before merging the update. Taking this release
puts the copy under these terms for 0.33.0 and every release after it.
What the copy took before stays under the MIT license it came with, and
nothing about that needs doing.

## The license (`LICENSE`, `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`, `package.json`), machinery

- **`LICENSE` is the Superforge Foundation License**, the whole file. A
  copy takes it as the template has it and never edits it: what the
  business adds is its own by term 3, with no notice of its own needed.
- **`THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` is new**: what came from others and stays
  under its own license. shadcn's components in
  `source/frontend/components/primitives/` (MIT, its notice in full), the
  rules `workbench/designing.md` adapts from Impeccable (Apache 2.0),
  and, used but not in the repository, the packages and DB-IP's database
  (CC BY 4.0).
- **`package.json` has `"license": "SEE LICENSE IN LICENSE"`.**

## What the docs say (`README.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `source/utilities/template.ts`, `workbench/scripts/smoke-test.mjs`), machinery

- **`README.md`'s "License"** says what the business may and may not do,
  and its opening paragraph adds that a business whose developer or
  agency builds for it makes the copy itself, under its own account or
  organization, and invites them to it.
- **`CLAUDE.md`'s `LICENSE` bullet** says what the license allows, and
  tells an agent asked to pass the codebase on, or to build on it for
  someone else from a copy of its own, to tell the owner what the
  license says first. A bullet for `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` joins it.
- **The generator credit is a term of the license**, not "the one thing
  Superforge asks": `CLAUDE.md`'s "Keep Superforge's credit" and its
  `source/utilities/` bullet, `template.ts`'s comment and the smoke
  test's message say so. The tag and its check are unchanged.
- **`START-HERE.md`**, which an update leaves out: step 0 stops a
  developer or an agency setting a copy up for a client in their own
  account, and step 5 keeps `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` with `LICENSE`.

## How to tell a copy already has it

`LICENSE`'s first line reads `Superforge Foundation License`.
