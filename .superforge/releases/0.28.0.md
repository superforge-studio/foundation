# 0.28.0 (2026-11-24)

The admin is rebuilt on Skylevel's frame: a sidebar of sections, who's
logged in at its foot with a gear beside them that opens Settings, and a
bar across the top on a phone. It opens on an Overview that says the
dashboard is the business's to build, and how. Settings holds the team,
read-only, in place of the Team screen. The Workflows screen is gone,
since a screen of the runs is one a business builds if it wants one: the
workflows, `/api/workflows` and the sample workflow are as they were. A
minor, since it changes the screens a copy may have built on. No flags.

## The frame (`components/dashboard/shell.tsx`, `navigation.ts`, `gate.tsx`, `app.ts`, `routes/admin/route.tsx`), machinery

- **A sidebar replaces the bar.** `DashboardFrame` draws the business's
  mark, the sections in their groups, and at the sidebar's foot who's
  logged in, their initials, name and role, which opens Your account,
  with the gear beside them, which opens Settings. On a phone it's a bar
  across the top with the mark, the gear and the initials, and every
  section in one row under it. The frame is the window's height, and each
  screen scrolls itself, as before.
- **The sections are `ADMIN_NAVIGATION`** (`navigation.ts`): the
  Overview, then the app's own groups, `APP_NAVIGATION` in
  `components/dashboard/app.ts`, a new `app.ts` beside the template's
  list, empty as the template ships. Each section has its address, name,
  icon (`lucide-react`) and area, and `shownTo` and `adminSectionAt` hide
  it from a role that can't read its area, and draw `NotPermitted` if they
  open it anyway, as `SECTIONS` did. `SECTIONS` and `DashboardBar` are
  gone. The sections are apart from the frame, so a screen that draws
  with the frame never imports the app's own sections back through it.
- **`gate.tsx`**: `DashboardLoading`, the frame's shape while the session
  is asked for, and `DashboardLogin`, the way in at the address that was
  opened, which `route.tsx` drew itself before.
- **`BackToList`'s `to` takes any address**, so a screen of the app's own
  needs no line in it. `Panes`, `SinglePane`, `ListLoading`,
  `CouldNotLoad`, `NotPermitted` and `AdminNotFound` are as they were;
  `AdminNotFound` links back to the Overview.
- `components/dashboard/README.md` is new: the frame, the template's
  screens, what stands in front of them, and the steps that add a
  section. `workbench/writing-code.md`'s "Yours and the template's" lists
  the new `app.ts`.

**A copy that added sections** to `SECTIONS` puts each in
`APP_NAVIGATION` instead, in a group (`{ group: "Customers", sections:
[…] }`), with an icon, and drops any address it added to `BackToList`'s
`to`. Its screens need nothing else. **A copy that restyled the bar**
puts its look on the sidebar, which is the frame now.

## The Overview (`routes/admin/index.tsx`, `components/dashboard/overview.tsx`), a starting point

`/admin` opens on the Overview, for everyone on the team, rather than
sending them to the first section their role reads. It says each section
is a screen, routes in the API and a table in the database, which Claude
Code builds when it's asked; writes that prompt for a section of the
business's own from what it keeps track of; and suggests one for what the
app already has with no screen, the team's files and the workflows' runs,
each gone once a section in the sidebar shows its area, and the site's
visits on the Overview itself. A copy makes it the home its team wants to
open on. `prompt.tsx` is new beside it: `CopyPrompt`, a button that copies
a prompt for Claude Code, `PromptChip`, a prompt in quotes that copies
itself, and `Step`.

## Settings (`routes/admin/settings.tsx`, `components/dashboard/settings/`), machinery

- **Settings is a page of parts**, each a `SettingsPart` (`key`, `area`
  and a component that asks the API for its own data), each shown to a
  role that may read its area. The gear shows to a role that may read one
  of them, and Settings draws `NotPermitted` for anyone else. The
  template's part is the team; the app's own follow it, `APP_SETTINGS` in
  `app.ts`.
- **The team** (`settings/team.tsx`) is the Team screen's list, as the
  code lists it: owners first, then the rest, then those taken off, with
  how to bring them back; each with their role (`RoleMark`, `marks.tsx`)
  and when they last logged in. Under it, what each role may do in each
  area, side by side (`RoleLevels`, `permissions.tsx`). **Add someone**,
  for a role that may write the `team` area, the owner's as the template
  ships, writes the prompt that has Claude Code list someone in
  `access.ts`, since nothing on a screen changes the team.

## Your account (`components/dashboard/account.tsx`), a starting point

"Your details" shows who someone's listed as: their first and last names,
their slug, the address they log in with and their role, and an owner gets
a prompt that has Claude Code change them. The rest of the page is as it
was.

## No Workflows screen, and no Team screen, a starting point

- **Gone**: `routes/admin/workflows.tsx`, `routes/admin/team.tsx`,
  `components/dashboard/runs.tsx`, `components/dashboard/team.tsx`, and
  `source/frontend/queries/workflows.ts`, with what only they used: the
  run marks in `marks.tsx` (`Stop`, `runStop`, `RUN_STATUS_NAMES`,
  `RunStatusMark`, `RunLine`, `StepRail`, `LiveNote`) and `took` in
  `words.ts`. `marks.tsx` keeps `useNow()` and gains `RoleMark`.
- **`ROLE_NAMES` moved** from `permissions.tsx` to `words.ts`, with
  `roleName`.
- **`/admin/workflows` and `/admin/team` answer the admin's 404**, in its
  frame, so a bookmark to either lands there. The smoke test probes
  `/admin` instead.
- **What still names the runs** says the API's list of runs now:
  `quiet`'s comment and the workflows' README ("Reading runs"), the jobs,
  files and webhooks READMEs, the seed's comment, `README.md`,
  `START-HERE.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and the home page's line map.

**A copy that kept its Workflows screen**, or built on it, keeps those
files as its own: `routes/admin/workflows.tsx`, `runs.tsx`,
`queries/workflows.ts`, and the run marks and `took`, moved into a file of
its own, since `marks.tsx` and `words.ts` are the template's. It gives the
screen a line in `APP_NAVIGATION` (`/admin/workflows`, area `workflows`),
and its `BackToList` its new type. The same goes for a copy that kept the
Team screen, at its own address, since `/admin/settings` has the team.

How to tell a copy already has it: `source/frontend/components/dashboard/app.ts`
exists, and `grep -rn "DashboardBar\|SECTIONS" source/frontend` finds
nothing.
