# 0.18.0 (2026-11-23)

Only the team logs in, and each role on it may do what the code gives it,
area by area. Foundation is the business's own admin: its customers don't
have accounts here. The team is listed in `access.ts`, as before, now each
by a slug that never changes, with a role, `owner` or `member`. What each
role may do is a level, `none`, `read` or `write`, in each area of the app
(`team`, `workflows`, `jobs`), which every route of the business's says it
needs, and which the admin reads to leave out what someone can't use.
Claude and API tokens act as their person, and never do more than their
role. The dashboard is `/admin`, and an address that's no page keeps the
frame around it.

- **The team**, machinery. **Schema**: `users` is the team only. It gains
  `slug` (lowercase words joined by hyphens, unique, with a check) and
  `deactivated_at`, and `role` is no longer read or written: its default
  becomes `internal`, and the next release drops it, since the version
  still serving reads it. `access.ts`'s `internal_users` each have a
  `slug` and a `role` (`owner` or `member`, `TEAM_ROLES`, a member when
  it's left out), and `login` is gone: nobody but the team logs in. The
  server's start and each login make each listed person's row follow
  the list by their slug (`backend/users/sync.ts`'s `keepListed` and
  `syncInternalUsers`, which refuses a list with a mistake in it,
  `problemsWith`), and deactivate anyone taken off it, ending their
  sessions and keeping their row. The login middleware counts someone
  only while the list has them by their slug, and only while their row
  is active. The copy's generated migration fails on rows already there
  (`slug` is `NOT NULL`), so its SQL is written by hand, as the
  template's `0008_the-team.sql` is:

  ```sql
  ALTER TABLE "users" ADD COLUMN "slug" text;
  UPDATE "users" SET "slug" = "id"::text;
  -- squawk-ignore adding-not-nullable-field
  ALTER TABLE "users" ALTER COLUMN "slug" SET NOT NULL;
  -- squawk-ignore disallowed-unique-constraint, constraint-missing-not-valid
  ALTER TABLE "users" ADD CONSTRAINT "users_slug_unique" UNIQUE("slug");
  -- squawk-ignore constraint-missing-not-valid
  ALTER TABLE "users" ADD CONSTRAINT "users_slug_check" CHECK ("slug" ~ '^[a-z0-9]+(-[a-z0-9]+)*$');
  ALTER TABLE "users" ADD COLUMN "deactivated_at" timestamp (3) with time zone;
  ALTER TABLE "users" ALTER COLUMN "role" SET DEFAULT 'internal';
  UPDATE "users" SET "deactivated_at" = now() WHERE "role" = 'external';
  DELETE FROM "auth"."sessions"
  WHERE "user_id" IN (SELECT "id" FROM "users" WHERE "deactivated_at" IS NOT NULL);
  ```

  Each row's slug starts as its id, which no list gives anyone, so the
  server's start finds each listed person by their address and gives
  their row the slug they're listed with. Everyone who was `external` is
  deactivated, logged out, and keeps their row.
- **Who logs in**, machinery: `backend/auth/login.ts` refuses anyone the
  list doesn't have (403 `not-listed`), before Infra is asked to email
  them. Logging in records no `signed-up` event any more, and queues no
  `user-signed-up`: a team member is never a sign-up. The People
  screen's adding, changing and removing, `POST`, `PATCH` and `DELETE
  /api/users`, are gone, and so is `PATCH /api/account`: a team member's
  name is the one they're listed with.
- **Permissions**, machinery: `source/utilities/permissions.ts` has the
  areas (`AREA_NAMES`, `AREAS`), the levels (`LEVELS`), a role's `Grant`
  (`"everything"`, or a level for each area it may use, the rest `none`)
  and `allows()`, which both sides check with. `access.ts`'s `roles` gives
  each role its grant: `owner` `"everything"`, `member` every area the
  template has. `backend/auth/logged-in.ts`'s `permitted(area, level?)`
  replaces `internalOnly`: a `GET` needs `read`, anything else `write`,
  unless the route says; someone whose role doesn't reach it gets 403
  `not-permitted` with `area` and `level` (both new, optional fields of
  the failure's shape, `utilities/schemas/failures.ts`), and a token that
  wasn't given it, 403 `read-only`. It puts what the request may do on
  `context.var.permissions`. `loggedIn` is for what's someone's own, like
  their account and their tokens; `internalOnly` is gone. Every route
  under `/api` says who may call it, which `backend/tests/routes.test.ts`
  checks. **A copy's own routes that take `internalOnly`** take
  `permitted("<area>")`, with an area of the copy's own added to
  `AREA_NAMES` and `AREAS` where none fits, and given to `member` in
  `roles`; a route that reads `context.var.user.role` reads `"owner"` or
  `"member"` now.
- **Tokens**, machinery: Claude's token and an API token act as their
  person, found by their slug, while they're listed and active, with
  their role's permissions narrowed by the token's: `read` reads what the
  role reads, and `act` changes what the role changes. The MCP's
  `list_routes` leaves out what the person's role can't reach, and says
  the area and level of each route it lists.
- **The session**, machinery: `GET /api/auth/session` and
  `POST /api/auth/verify` answer `permissions` beside `user`, and `user`
  has the `slug`, `first_name`, `last_name` and `role` the list gives
  them. `useSession()` hands both to every screen.
- **The admin**, a starting point: `source/frontend/routes/dashboard/` is
  `routes/admin/`, at `/admin`, with no redirect from `/dashboard`. The
  bar's sections (`SECTIONS`, `components/dashboard/shell.tsx`) each name
  their area, and someone whose role can't read it doesn't see it, or
  gets `NotPermitted` if they open it. The People screen is the Team
  screen (`routes/admin/team.tsx`, `components/dashboard/team.tsx`): the
  team as the code lists it, and those taken off it, each with their role
  and what it lets them do, and nothing that changes anyone. The account
  page shows "What you can do" (`components/dashboard/permissions.tsx`,
  with `ROLE_NAMES`) and no longer edits the name. A run's Try again shows
  only to a role that may write the workflows. `vite.config.ts`'s
  prerender filter, `source/server.ts`'s traced route (`/admin/*`), the
  login page's link and the smoke test follow. A copy that changed its
  dashboard keeps its screens and moves them under `routes/admin/`.
- **404s that keep their surroundings**, a starting point: an address
  under `/admin` that's no page is answered in the admin's frame
  (`AdminNotFound`, the admin route's `notFoundComponent`), and anywhere
  else by `components/states/not-found.tsx`, now with the business's
  mark over it.
- **The sample workflow**, a starting point: `tell-team-of-new-member`
  (`backend/users/workflows.ts`), on the new `user-added` event, which
  the sync queues when it makes someone's row, tells the rest of the team,
  in the logs. `tell-team-of-sign-up` and its `user-signed-up` event stay
  listed until the next release, for runs a copy had in flight, and
  nothing starts them. The seed starts runs of the new one.
- **The seed**, machinery: `seed/users.ts` seeds the team from
  `access.ts` with their slugs, and someone taken off it, deactivated;
  no customers. The sample visit that ended in a sign-up ends in one of
  the team logging in.
- **The docs**, machinery: `CLAUDE.md`'s architecture list,
  `workbench/writing-code.md` ("Headless", "The team and what each role
  may do", which replaces "Roles and who can log in"), `START-HERE.md`
  (who's on the team, and whether anyone should see less), `README.md`,
  and the READMEs of `backend/jobs/`, `backend/mcp/`,
  `backend/workflows/`, `backend/visits/`, `frontend/visitors/` and
  `components/toolbar/`.

**Schema**: the migration above, written by hand.

**Owner**: give each person in `access.ts`'s `internal_users` a `slug`
(their first name, lowercase) and the owner `role: "owner"`, and take out
`login`. Anyone who logged in as a customer can't any more, and is
deactivated as the update deploys.

**How to tell a copy already has it**: `source/utilities/permissions.ts`
exists, `schema.ts`'s `users` has `slug`, and `source/frontend/routes/admin/`
exists.
