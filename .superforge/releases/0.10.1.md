# 0.10.1 (2026-11-22)

Every PR to the template is a release, and CI checks it. Until now a PR a
copy wouldn't take, like a change to the template's own README, went out
with no version of its own, and nothing stopped two PRs made side by side
from claiming the same one. Nothing in the app changes.

- **What each number means**, machinery: `workbench/updating.md`'s "How
  a release is written" now counts a small improvement as a patch, beside
  a bug fix, and says the major moves only when Superforge says so.
- **`release-version`**, a CI job, machinery, though a copy skips it: on
  a PR to the template, it checks that the version in
  `.superforge/superforge.json` is the one right after the base's (a
  patch, a minor or a major), with its notes in `.superforge/releases/`
  and its line in `CHANGELOG.md`. `check` waits for it, and passes when
  it's skipped, as it is in a copy and on Dependabot's PRs, whose changes
  go out with the next release. A copy's `.github/workflows/ci.yml` takes
  the job and `check`'s new step as the template has them.
- The template's own `README.md`, "This template", "Releases", says every
  PR is a release, even one a copy has nothing to take from. A copy has
  removed that section, so there's nothing in it to take.

How to tell a copy already has it: a `release-version` job in
`.github/workflows/ci.yml`.
