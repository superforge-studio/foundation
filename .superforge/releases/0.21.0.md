# 0.21.0 (2026-11-23)

A file has one owner now: the template, or the app. Wherever the template
keeps a list an app adds to, the tables, the routes, the workflows, the
app's own entries go in a file of the app's, named `app.ts`, beside the
template's, which reads it. The template made each empty, and no later
release changes one, so what a business adds stops meeting each update as
a conflict. Login gets the same: one place for someone besides the team to
log in, which the template leaves empty. Nothing the app does changes, and
no table does.

**After this update, move what the copy added to the template's lists
into its `app.ts` files**, each below. Until it does, the update's
conflicts are in exactly those files, and the next release's will be too.

- **Tables**, machinery. `source/database/schema.ts` is now the
  conventions and two exports: the template's tables moved, as they were,
  to `source/database/schema/foundation.ts`, and the app's own go in
  `source/database/schema/app.ts`, new and empty. The code still imports
  every table from `@/database/schema`. `auth` and `internal`, the
  Postgres schemas, are in `source/database/schema/namespaces.ts`, which a
  table in one imports them from. A copy with tables of its own in
  `schema.ts`: take the release's `schema.ts` and `schema/foundation.ts`,
  then move each of the copy's tables to `schema/app.ts`, with its imports
  (`../columns`, `./foundation` for a table it points at, `./namespaces`,
  and `../../utilities/…`, one folder deeper than before). **No migration**:
  `pnpm database:generate` should then say "No schema changes".
- **Routes and what they see on `context`**, machinery.
  `source/backend/app.ts`, new: `appRoutes`, a Hono app the template's API
  mounts after its own (`source/backend/index.ts`), `AppVariables` and
  `appVariables()`, what the app's routes see on `context` beside the
  template's (`ApiEnvironment`, `source/backend/context.ts`), and
  `takesFormsFromOtherSites`, for a route that must take another site's
  form. `index.ts` puts each variable on `context` by its name, and exports
  `serverVariables()`. A copy that mounted its own routes in `index.ts`,
  or added to `context.ts`: move each `.route("/", …)` into `appRoutes`,
  and each variable into `AppVariables` and `appVariables`, with a test's
  stand-in for it in `source/backend/tests/support/app.ts`
  (`appTestVariables`), new, beside the routes anyone may call
  (`APP_ANYONE`), which the routes' test reads with the template's.
- **Workflows, runners and jobs**, machinery.
  `source/backend/workflows/app.ts` (`APP_WORKFLOWS`, `APP_RUNNERS`) and
  `source/backend/jobs/app.ts` (`APP_JOBS`), new, which `index.ts` in each
  folder takes after the template's. A copy's own move from `WORKFLOWS`,
  `RUNNERS` and `JOBS` to these.
- **Seeders**, machinery. `source/database/seed/app.ts`, new:
  `APP_SEEDERS`, run after the template's, `APP_UNSEEDED`, and
  `appSampleRuns`, the runs the app's sample rows would have started. A
  copy's own seeders and its `UNSEEDED` lines move there.
  `enqueueFromScript`'s runs are a named type, `ScriptRun`
  (`source/backend/workflows/engine.ts`).
- **Kinds of file**, machinery. The template's kinds are on
  `FoundationStorage` (`source/database/storage/files.ts`, the class that
  was `Storage`), and `Storage`, which every route still gets, is in
  `source/database/storage/app.ts`, new, and extends it. A copy's own kinds
  move there, listed after `super.kinds`.
- **Areas**, machinery. `source/utilities/permissions.ts` is now
  `source/utilities/permissions/index.ts`, imported from the same path,
  and the app's own areas go in `permissions/app.ts`, new, as
  `APP_AREAS`, each name with what it covers. `AREAS` is the two
  together, and `AREA_NAMES` its keys. A copy's own areas move there.
- **Someone besides the team who logs in**, machinery, new.
  `source/backend/auth/others.ts` says what the login routes ask of a
  second kind of person, a customer or a member: whether their address may
  start, logging them in once Infra says it's theirs, what
  `/api/auth/session` says of them, and ending their session.
  `source/backend/auth/app.ts` names who that is: `NO_ONE_ELSE`, as the
  template ships, so only the team logs in, as before. The routes ask it
  only of an address the team doesn't have
  (`source/backend/auth/routes.ts`). `loggedIn`, `permitted` and
  `auth.sessions` stay the team's alone: whoever else logs in keeps their
  sessions in a table of their own, with a check of their own.
  `sessionQuery` keeps the whole of what `/api/auth/session` answered
  (`SessionAnswer`, `source/frontend/queries/auth.ts`), and a login asks
  it again instead of working it out, so a page can read what the app
  says there beside the team member.
- **A check before the app says it's healthy**, machinery, new.
  `source/backend/health/app.ts`'s `appHealth`, which `/api/health` asks
  after the database answers. Nothing, as the template ships.
- **Answers that are neither a page nor the API**, machinery, new.
  `source/app.ts`: `appAnswer`, asked after `/api` and before the MCP and
  the pages, `appPageRoute`, what a page of the app's is traced as, and
  `HOSTS_WITHOUT_FILES`, hosts whose every path the server answers, which
  `source/node.ts` sends past the build's files. All empty as the template
  ships. `node.ts` renders through one function, `rendered`.
- **From Skylevel, which had each already**, machinery:
  `followAnswer` (`source/backend/visits/cookies.ts`), the cookie step
  after a visit is recorded, for any route that records one;
  `internalEmails(database, except?)`, with no one left out when a notice
  is about nobody on the team; and two tests, an IPv6 address counted the
  same however it's written, and the upgrade test's sample tables named so
  they never meet a copy's own.
- **The template's tests check its own entries**, machinery: a session's
  answer, a member's levels and the bucket's kinds are matched for what
  the template has, whatever an app adds, and `logInAs` says so when a
  login wasn't the team's. A stand-in in `tests/support/app.ts` leaves the
  template as it ships until a test says otherwise, so these hold in any
  app.
- **The docs**, machinery: `workbench/writing-code.md` gains "Yours and
  the template's", with every `app.ts` and what goes in it, and three
  rules that were Skylevel's alone: a table's name starts with what it's
  about, a row is keyed by a uuid Postgres makes, and a fixed list is
  `text({ enum })` with a check, never a Postgres enum. And "Each part's
  own guide" lists every directory's README by what it's for.

**How to tell a copy already has it**: `source/backend/app.ts` and
`source/database/schema/foundation.ts` exist, and `source/database/schema.ts`
ends with `export * from "./schema/foundation"`.
